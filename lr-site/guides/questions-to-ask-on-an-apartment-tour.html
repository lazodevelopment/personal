15 tour questions leasing agents hope you skip.

The tour is a sales presentation. These questions turn it back into an inspection.

By the LeaseReputation team · Updated 2026-07-18

Money — the real number, not the advertised one

  1. "What does the total move-in cost come to, all-in?" Application fee, admin fee, deposit, first month, pet deposit — get the single number in writing. Admin fees alone routinely add $150–500 and appear after you're emotionally committed.
  2. "What's the monthly total with every mandatory fee?" Valet trash, package lockers, "amenity fees," mandatory internet packages. A $1,500 apartment is often a $1,680 apartment.
  3. "How much did rent increase at renewal for current residents this year?" The move-in special is bait; the renewal increase is the business model. If they won't say, that's your answer.
  4. "Is the deposit refundable, and what were the top three deductions last year?" A confident property answers this easily.

Maintenance — the thing that actually determines your happiness

  1. "What's the average time to close a non-emergency maintenance request?" The single most predictive question on this list. "We're usually pretty quick" is not a number.
  2. "Is maintenance in-house or contracted?" In-house teams turn requests around in days; some contracted setups take weeks.
  3. "What's the after-hours emergency process?" Ask who actually answers at 11pm on a Saturday when the water heater fails.

The building's honest condition

  1. "Can I see the actual unit I'd rent — not the model?" Model units are staged theater. If your unit "isn't ready to show," ask why.
  2. "How old are the HVAC, water heater, and appliances in my unit?" A 15-year-old AC in Phoenix is a summer of emergency requests waiting to happen.
  3. "Any planned construction, renovation, or ownership changes?" New ownership almost always means new fees and new rules mid-lease.

The neighbors and the noise

  1. "What's the resident turnover rate?" High turnover is the building telling you something its marketing won't.
  2. "Can I come back at 9pm?" A property that says no to an evening walk-by has a reason. Parking lots, hallway noise, and the pool crowd all tell the truth after dark.
  3. "What's the wall construction between units?" Concrete between floors is a different life than wood-frame. Ask; agents know.

The exit

  1. "What exactly does breaking the lease cost?" Life happens — jobs move, relationships change. Know the number before you need it.
  2. "What are the renewal terms and notice requirements?" Some leases quietly auto-renew or require 60–90 days' notice; missing the window costs a month's rent.

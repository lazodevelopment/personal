Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 7, 2026

LeaseReputation is committed to making our Service usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to find out what living somewhere is really like. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we treat accessibility as an ongoing part of how we build.

Measures we take

  • Semantic, structured HTML and clear page hierarchy.
  • Keyboard navigability and visible focus indicators.
  • Color choices intended to meet contrast guidelines.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences.
  • Responsive layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images and icons.

Conformance status

We consider the Service to be partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 AA, meaning some content may not yet fully meet the standard. We are continually testing and improving, and accessibility is part of our ongoing work.

Known limitations

Some third-party content and embedded components may not be fully accessible and are partly outside our control. Where we identify issues, we work to address or replace them.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on LeaseReputation, we want to hear about it. Email info@leasereputation.com with the page and a description of the problem, and we will do our best to respond promptly and provide the information or function you need.

Good Questions

Everything, answered

The details couples ask about most — before they ever have to ask.

Do you really travel anywhere?

Yes — we're a national studio and travel is built into every collection. No surprise fees, no mileage math. If your day is happening, we'll be there.

How far out should we book?

Most couples reserve 9–18 months ahead; peak Saturdays go first. Booking is what holds the date — inquiries alone don't.

Photo, film, or both?

Both, if the budget allows — they answer different questions. Photos hold the details; film holds the voices. Our combined collections are priced to make choosing both the easy call.

How many images do we get?

A full wedding typically delivers several hundred fully edited images — every keeper, not a teaser set — in a private online gallery you can download and share.

When is everything delivered?

Sneak peeks within days; complete galleries and films on a clearly stated timeline in your agreement — we hit our dates.

Do we get the raw footage?

Ask us — we're one of the few studios happy to talk about raw files rather than pretend they don't exist.

What if our venue is somewhere you've never shot?

Then we scout it before your wedding — light, sound, logistics — so the day itself has zero improvisation.

How do we reserve our date?

Check availability through the form; once we confirm the date is open, a signed agreement locks it in — pay in full at the listed price, or choose our plan: add $500, 50% down at booking, balance due two weeks before your wedding.

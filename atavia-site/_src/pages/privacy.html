Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Atavia Weddings LLC ("Atavia Weddings," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through ataviaweddings.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, wedding date, venue, and any details you share — when you complete our booking or contact form, or otherwise communicate with us. We also automatically collect limited technical information (such as browser type, device, and pages visited) through standard web analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and prepare quotes and proposals;
  • To schedule, coordinate, and deliver photography and videography services;
  • To send booking confirmations, invoices, and service updates;
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

Cookies & Analytics

Our site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and measure traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate — for example, our form provider, website host, and payment processor — and only as needed to provide our services, or when required by law. Embedded content from third parties (such as YouTube or Instagram) is subject to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services and for legitimate business or legal purposes. You may request that we delete information we hold about you, subject to any records we are required to keep.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

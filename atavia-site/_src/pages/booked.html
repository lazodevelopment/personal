What Happens Next

Three things to expect.

  1. Your receipt and welcome email. Your retainer payment is applied to your booking. Your receipt and a welcome note from info@ataviaweddings.com arrive within a few minutes — check spam if they don’t.
  2. Your remaining balance. If you chose the standard plan, the balance is charged to the same card 14 days after signing, exactly as your agreement states, with a heads-up email the day before. Paid in full? There is nothing else to pay.
  3. Your wedding-day questionnaire. As your date gets closer we’ll send a short questionnaire — timeline, key people, the moments that matter most to you — so the team arrives already knowing your day.

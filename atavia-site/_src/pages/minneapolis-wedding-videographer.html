Good to Know

Minneapolis wedding videography questions.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Minneapolis?

Atavia film collections start at $1,200 and photography at $1,500, with no travel fees anywhere in the Twin Cities. A $500 deposit reserves your date, and every collection includes your raw footage.

Do you charge travel fees for Minneapolis weddings?

No. A local Atavia team covers the Twin Cities, so there is no out-of-town crew and no travel line on your invoice.

Can one team cover both photo and video?

Yes. Our Photo & Film collections put your photographer and filmmaker on one team with one plan for the day, so nobody is in the other’s shot. See packages.

Will we get the raw footage?

Yes. Every collection includes complimentary access to your raw footage alongside the edited film.

How many hours of coverage does a Minneapolis wedding need?

Collections run 4, 6, 8 or 10 hours. Eight hours is the most popular: it covers the end of getting ready, the ceremony, portraits, and the reception through the first dances. Add hours à la carte when you book if your timeline runs longer.

Is a second shooter worth it?

For weddings of eight hours or more, or with a large wedding party, usually yes. A second photographer or videographer is in two places at once — both of you getting ready, key reactions during the vows, wide and intimate angles together — and brings backup gear. It is a $500 add-on on any collection.

How does booking work?

Choose your collection on our booking page, sign the agreement online, and a $500 retainer is charged the moment you sign. Your remaining balance is charged automatically 14 days after signing, or you can pay in full up front.

Are you insured?

Fully insured, for your peace of mind and your venue’s, and we carry backup cameras and audio on every wedding.

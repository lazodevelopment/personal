Before You Reach Out

Questions,
Answered

The details couples ask about most — deposits, travel, turnaround, and everything in between.

Is there a deposit to reserve our date?
A nominal $500 deposit reserves your date, and it's applied toward your package price. Dates are held first-come, first-served.
Is there a travel fee?
No travel fees. We keep dedicated teams positioned in your area, so you get seamless service without extra travel costs. If your wedding is less than 45 days out, a $250 accommodation fee applies.
What's your most popular package?
Our 8-hour coverage is by far the most popular. It strikes a well-rounded balance, capturing everything from getting ready through the major reception moments.
Can we receive the raw footage?
Yes — we provide complimentary access to all raw, unedited footage. You'll get a private link to view and download everything at your convenience.
Is drone footage included?
Drone footage is included in our larger packages. Our licensed operators capture aerial views of your venue when weather, FAA regulations, and timing permit. For safety and discretion, drones are never flown during the ceremony.
What's the turnaround time?
Standard editing is 30–90 days after your wedding, with most films completed within 30–60 days. Peak season can extend that, and a rush option is available for an additional fee.
How long is the engagement session?
Engagement sessions run one hour and can include multiple nearby locations within that time, giving you a variety of looks and settings.
Are you insured?
Yes — we are fully insured, giving you and your venue added peace of mind. We also carry backup cameras, lenses, and essential gear to every event.
How do we book?
Choose your collection on the Packages page, then complete the booking form on our Book Now page. A $500 deposit reserves your date and is applied toward your total.

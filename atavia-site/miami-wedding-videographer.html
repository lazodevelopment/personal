Good to Know

Miami wedding videography questions.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Miami?

Atavia film collections start at $1,200 and photography at $1,400, with no travel fees anywhere in South Florida. A $500 deposit reserves your date, and every collection includes your raw footage.

Do you charge travel fees for Miami weddings?

No. A local Atavia team covers South Florida, so there is no out-of-town crew and no travel line on your invoice.

Can one team cover both photo and video?

Yes. Our Photo & Film collections put your photographer and filmmaker on one team with one plan for the day, so nobody is in the other’s shot. See packages.

Will we get the raw footage?

Yes. Every collection includes complimentary access to your raw footage alongside the edited film.

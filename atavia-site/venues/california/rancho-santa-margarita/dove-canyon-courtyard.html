Rancho Santa Margarita, California

Your day at Dove Canyon Courtyard.

Dove Canyon Courtyard is a banquet and reception venue. It is the kind of place that gives a wedding film and a photo gallery real atmosphere — and we would love to capture yours there.

You get a local photography and film team — no travel fees, no out-of-town crew flying in. Every collection includes complimentary access to your raw footage, and a $500 deposit reserves your date.

Sixteen years and 1,300+ weddings have taken our teams through ballrooms, barns, beaches, and vineyards. If we haven’t filmed at Dove Canyon Courtyard yet, we scout it before your wedding — the light, the sound, the timeline — so nothing about the room surprises us on the day.

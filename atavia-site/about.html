About Atavia Weddings

The Heart
Behind Atavia

Storytellers who believe your day deserves to be remembered exactly as it felt — present, unhurried, and beautifully real.

At Atavia Weddings, we blend elegance, artistry, and authenticity to preserve every meaningful moment of your day — from quiet glances to grand celebrations. Backed by years of experience, our team crafts breathtaking imagery and cinematic films thoughtfully tailored to reflect your unique vision, so the memories you make today remain just as vivid decades from now.

As a nationwide team and featured vendor on The Knot, WeddingWire, and Zola, we proudly serve couples across the country with photography, videography, and combined collections designed to capture your day from every angle. Whether you want stunning stills, a cinematic film, or the complete story told through both, we tailor our services to fit your vision and your venue — wherever your wedding takes you.

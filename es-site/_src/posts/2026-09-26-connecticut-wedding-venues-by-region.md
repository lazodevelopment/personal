Connecticut is small enough to drive across in two hours and varied enough that a Greenwich harbor wedding and a Litchfield farm wedding share almost nothing except the sunset time. Here are the venues couples ask us about most in each part of the state, and the timeline numbers that apply to all of them.

## The light, statewide

Connecticut is compact, so the sun sets within about four minutes of the same time everywhere in the state. These times are computed for Hartford on the 15th of each month in 2027; Greenwich runs three minutes later, Mystic a minute earlier.

| Month | Start ceremony | Golden hour | Sunset | Daylight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| January | 1:59 PM | 4:01 PM | 4:44 PM | 9.5 h |
| February | 2:38 PM | 4:44 PM | 5:23 PM | 10.6 h |
| March | 4:12 PM | 6:20 PM | 6:57 PM | 11.9 h |
| April | 4:46 PM | 6:53 PM | 7:31 PM | 13.3 h |
| May | 5:18 PM | 7:23 PM | 8:03 PM | 14.5 h |
| June | 5:42 PM | 7:45 PM | 8:27 PM | 15.2 h |
| July | 5:38 PM | 7:42 PM | 8:23 PM | 14.9 h |
| August | 5:05 PM | 7:12 PM | 7:50 PM | 13.9 h |
| September | 4:15 PM | 6:23 PM | 7:00 PM | 12.5 h |
| October | 3:24 PM | 5:31 PM | 6:09 PM | 11.1 h |
| November | 1:45 PM | 3:48 PM | 4:30 PM | 9.8 h |
| December | 1:35 PM | 3:35 PM | 4:20 PM | 9.2 h |

The start-ceremony column is sunset minus two hours and forty-five minutes, which leaves vows, family formals and a portrait walk inside golden hour. October, the most requested month in the Litchfield Hills, means a 3:30 ceremony. Couples who set a 5:00 October ceremony because it sounded right are photographing portraits in the dark.

## What we would do

For a summer wedding anywhere in the state, start the ceremony between 5:30 and 5:45 and walk out for portraits at 7:45. For a peak foliage Saturday in the Litchfield Hills, start at 3:30, do a first look at 1:30 so formals are done before the ceremony, and be outside with the last light at 5:30. Every venue above has its own page with the sunset table for that exact spot and our published pricing, which is the same at all of them.

El Paso has the most reliable wedding light in Texas: clear skies most of the year, a desert horizon, and the Franklin Mountains standing in the middle of the city catching the last of the sun. It also has a set of venues that do not look like anything in Austin or Dallas. Here are the eight couples ask us about and how the light works at each.

One El Paso-specific note: the mountains. A venue on the west side of the Franklins loses direct sun earlier than the table says in the morning, and one on the east side loses it earlier in the evening. The downtown venues sit south of the range and get the full evening. We check the exact angle on the scout.

**St. Rogers Depot** is the venue couples search for by name, and it deserves it: brick, timber, and the proportions of a building that was built to hold a crowd. It photographs best in the hour before the table's ceremony time, when the low sun comes through the west-facing openings. **Epic Railyard** is its rougher industrial cousin, with the kind of scale and texture that a film crew loves and that needs off-camera lighting done properly. **Keystone Heritage Park** is the outdoor choice, a botanical garden with desert planting and a wetland, and it is the one venue on the list where the ceremony time in the table is not negotiable, because there is nowhere to hide from the sun if you start too early.

The five ballrooms are where most El Paso weddings actually happen. They solve the light problem entirely for the ceremony and reception, and the question becomes where the fifteen-minute outdoor portrait session goes. Vienna has its garden. For the others, we scout a spot within five minutes for the sunset walk, and the Scenic Drive overlook and the edge of the desert are both close enough.

## Big weddings

El Paso weddings run large, and the ballrooms are built for three hundred. That is second-photographer territory. One camera cannot be at the grand entrance and on the parents' faces at the same time, and we would rather say so now. The second photographer is $500 on any collection.

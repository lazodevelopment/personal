## Providence and Narragansett Bay

The east shore of the bay faces west across the water, which means the venues in East Providence and Warwick get the sunset over the bay that Newport, on the other side, does not.

Squantum is a private club on a point of land sticking into the bay, and it has one of the best west-facing ceremony lawns in the state. The Aldrich Mansion, a seventy-room house on seventy acres of Warwick shoreline, is the grandest thing outside Newport. The Providence G is the downtown choice for a city wedding with hotels in walking distance. [Belle Mer](/venues/providence-ri/belle-mer-a-longwood-venue) and [Newport Beach House](/venues/providence-ri/newport-beach-house-a-longwood-venue) also appear in Providence searches; they are Newport venues and are covered in that post. ## Block Island

The island is twelve miles out, reached by ferry from Point Judith in about an hour on the traditional boat or thirty minutes on the fast one. Everything about the wedding follows from that.

| Venue | Google rating | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Spring House Hotel](/venues/block-island-ri/spring-house-hotel) | 4.5 (617) | The island's big hotel, on the hill above Old Harbor |
| [Block Island Beach House](/venues/block-island-ri/block-island-beach-house) | 4.1 (466) | On Crescent Beach |
| [The Sullivan House](/venues/block-island-ri/the-sullivan-house) | 4.6 (68) | Inn with a lawn |
| [Kinney Bungalow at Sunset Farm](/venues/block-island-ri/kinney-bungalow-at-sunset-farm) | 4.4 (107) | Narragansett, on the mainland near the ferry |
| [Regatta Place](/venues/block-island-ri/regatta-place) | 4.9 (107) | Newport, not on the island |

The Spring House is the wedding venue on Block Island, a Victorian hotel with a lawn looking over the water, and its rooms usually hold most of the guest list. That is the logistics answer: guests come out on Friday, the wedding is Saturday, everyone leaves Sunday, and the ferry schedule is the timeline's fixed point. Vendors, including us, come out the day before. Build the extra night into the plan and the island stops being a complication.

Kinney Bungalow and Regatta Place appear in Block Island searches because they are near the ferry or across the water. They are not on the island. [Block Island hub](/venues/block-island-ri/).

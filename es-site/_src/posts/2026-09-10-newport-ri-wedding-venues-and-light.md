Newport is small enough that five of the venues on this list are within a mile of Bowen's Wharf, and that changes the shape of the day. Getting ready in a Thames Street inn, walking to the ceremony, and a reception with the harbor out the window is not a fantasy here; it is the default. The variable is which way the water faces, because on an island the sunset is only over the water from one side.

## Which side of the island

The harbor side faces west. [Belle Mer](/venues/newport-ri/belle-mer-a-longwood-venue) and [Regatta Place](/venues/newport-ri/regatta-place) on Goat Island look back at the town and the Pell Bridge, and the sun sets behind the bridge into Narragansett Bay; if you want the classic over-the-water sunset with sailboats in it, this is the side. [OceanCliff Hotel](/venues/newport-ri/oceancliff-hotel) on Ocean Drive looks southwest over the bay's mouth and gets the same sunset with fewer masts. The ocean side faces southeast. [The Chanler at Cliff Walk](/venues/newport-ri/the-chanler-at-cliff-walk) sits above Easton's Beach, and [Newport Beach House](/venues/newport-ri/newport-beach-house-a-longwood-venue) is on the beach itself; the sun rises out of the sea there and sets behind you, over the town, so the evening light is a soft glow on the water rather than a direct sunset, and a first-light session the next morning is the shot. [Glen Manor House](/venues/newport-ri/glen-manor-house) in Portsmouth is on the Sakonnet River side of the island, quiet and green, with the water to the east. [Queen Anne's Loft](/venues/newport-ri/queen-anne-s-loft) and [Little Gold](/venues/newport-ri/little-gold) are in-town rooms a few blocks from the wharf, for couples who want the ceremony at a church or on the Cliff Walk and the party close by.

Our rule for an outdoor ceremony is simple: **start it two hours and forty-five minutes before sunset.** Vows take twenty to thirty minutes, family formals another thirty, and that leaves a portrait walk that lands in golden hour with the reception opening as the light goes. Move the ceremony later than that and portraits happen in the dark; earlier and you are photographing in flat, overhead light with the good hour wasted on cocktail hour.

In June that puts the ceremony at 5:35 PM with golden hour opening at 7:38 PM; in September, one of Newport's most-booked months, the ceremony moves up to 4:09 PM. Note that the Cliff Walk and the beach are public and busy from Memorial Day to Labor Day, so a portrait walk there is a weekday-morning or a September thing.

The Panhandle and the South Plains have the latest sunsets in Texas and the biggest skies, and a wedding here in June ends its ceremony at nine in full daylight. Amarillo and Lubbock are two hours apart, share the same light and the same weather, and each has a set of venues couples ask us about by name. Here they are, with the timeline numbers for both cities.

## The light on the High Plains

A 9:03 PM June sunset is the latest on this site outside the far north. The practical effect is that a summer wedding here can start its ceremony at 6:30, run a full cocktail hour, and still have the couple outside for portraits at 8:30 in the best light of the year. The other effect is the sky itself. With no hills and no trees to speak of, the horizon is the whole picture, and a Panhandle sunset over a ranch venue does more for a portrait than any backdrop we could bring.

Wind is the caveat. It blows most afternoons in spring, and an outdoor ceremony in April needs a plan for the veil, the programs and the arbor.

Garden of Dreams is the venue couples search for most in Amarillo, a garden property on the northwest side with a 4.9 rating, and it is a good example of why the ceremony time matters here: a garden ceremony at 6:18 in June is in full sun with no shade tall enough to help, and we would move it to seven. Starlight Canyon carries the most reviews and the canyon-country setting that the rest of the flat Panhandle cannot offer. Cornerstone Ranch and Knotting Hill are the two ranch-style venues where the horizon does the work. All eight are on the [Amarillo hub](/venues/amarillo-tx/).

Lubbock's two most interesting venues are downtown: the Baker Building and the Watson Building, both restored early-twentieth-century commercial buildings a few blocks apart with the brick, the tall windows and the ceiling height that make an indoor reception look like a film set. Both need the fifteen-minute sunset walk planned, because downtown Lubbock does not give you the horizon; we have a spot. Kitalou Gin is the opposite, a restored cotton gin out in the fields with the whole sky behind it. The [Lubbock hub](/venues/lubbock-tx/) has all eight with pricing.

## What we would do

June at a ranch venue: ceremony at 7:00, cocktail hour with the sun still up, and the two of you out on the property at 8:30 for the last half hour of a sunset that fills the frame edge to edge. October: 4:30, and a first look at 2:30 to keep formals off the good light. A winter downtown Lubbock wedding: ceremony at 3:00, and the Baker Building's windows do the rest.

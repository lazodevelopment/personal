The most common timeline mistake on the Cape is a ceremony set for the same hour whether the wedding is in June or October. The sun does not cooperate with that. In mid-June it sets over the Cape at 8:16 PM; by mid-October it is gone at 5:59 PM, and by mid-November, after the clocks change, it is down at 4:20 PM. Same venue, same couple, a two-hour difference in when the day has to start.

Our rule for an outdoor ceremony is simple: **start it two hours and forty-five minutes before sunset.** Vows take twenty to thirty minutes, family formals another thirty, and that leaves a portrait walk that lands in golden hour with the reception opening as the light goes. Move the ceremony later than that and portraits happen in the dark; earlier and you are photographing in flat, overhead light with the good hour wasted on cocktail hour.

Three things stand out. June and July are almost identical, so a summer Saturday can start at 5:30 and still finish portraits with light. September, the Cape's favorite month after the crowds thin, has already lost an hour and a half of evening. And anything after the first weekend of November is a one o'clock ceremony if you want daylight portraits at all, which is worth knowing before you fall in love with a late-autumn date.

## Which way your venue faces

On the Cape the water is on three sides, so the question is not whether you will have a waterfront sunset but whether the sun goes down over the water or over the land behind you. Bay-side venues on the north shore, like [Ocean Edge Resort & Golf Club](/venues/cape-cod-ma/ocean-edge-resort-golf-club) in Brewster, look west-northwest across Cape Cod Bay in summer and get the sun setting into the water. Sound-side venues on the south shore, [Wychmere Beach Club](/venues/cape-cod-ma/wychmere-beach-club) in Harwich Port and [Pelham House Resort](/venues/cape-cod-ma/pelham-house-resort) in Dennis Port, have the water in front of them and the sunset off to one side, which is a beautiful thing in its own right: the sky colors over the Sound while the light on the beach stays soft rather than blinding. [Wequassett Resort and Golf Club](/venues/cape-cod-ma/wequassett-resort-and-golf-club) sits on Pleasant Bay facing east, so the evening light there is reflected glow rather than a direct sunset. We scout the exact angle before the wedding; it decides where the ceremony chairs go.

## What we would do

For a summer wedding, start at 5:30, do family formals right after the recessional, and walk out for portraits at 7:34 PM while cocktail hour runs. For September, start at 4:00 and do a first look at 2:30 so you are not spending golden hour on formals. For October and later, start at 3:00 or earlier, or accept that portraits will be in the last light and plan the reception to be lit the way it should be filmed. Whichever month it is, the peak-season table is on our [Cape Cod page](/wedding-photographer/cape-cod-ma) as well, and every venue above has its own page with sunset and pricing for that exact spot.

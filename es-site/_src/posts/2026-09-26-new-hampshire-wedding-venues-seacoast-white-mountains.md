New Hampshire gives you three different weddings inside a ninety-minute drive: a harbor and a grand hotel on the Seacoast, a mountain valley in the north, and the barns and vineyards of the Merrimack Valley in the middle. The sunset is nearly the same across all three; the light is not.

## The light

Computed for the 15th of each month in 2027. Portsmouth and Manchester are within three minutes of each other; North Conway is four minutes behind Portsmouth in June, and the mountains take the sun off the valley floor earlier than the table says.

In the White Mountains, subtract another twenty to thirty minutes from the ceremony start if your site is in a valley with a ridge to the west. The sun goes behind the ridge before it sets, and the golden hour becomes a golden forty minutes.

## What we would do

A June Seacoast wedding: ceremony at 5:30, portraits on the water at 7:40, and a reception that still has light at nine. A foliage wedding in Jackson: first look at 1:00, ceremony at 3:15, and the reception lit for film. A September barn wedding near Concord: ceremony at 4:00, family formals at 4:30, and a twenty-minute walk into the field at 6:15.

Photographing a wedding at Willowdale Estate

Estates and gardens give a wedding rooms and grounds in the same afternoon. We map the property during scouting — which lawn holds shade at four, which room has window light for getting ready — and run the timeline so nothing is a long walk from the next photograph.

From harbor light to foliage season, Boston weddings carry a classic register — historic venues, formal timelines, family traditions. We know how to run an efficient formals session and still disappear into the candid moments. If we haven’t worked Willowdale Estate yet we scout it before your day — light, timing, logistics — so nothing about the venue surprises us on the morning of.

At Willowdale Estate the sun sets at 8:23 PM in June and 6:01 PM in October, with golden hour opening around 7:40 PM on a June evening and 6:16 PM in late September. Start a summer ceremony near 5:38 PM and you will still have the good light for portraits; in autumn move it an hour earlier. The full month-by-month table is on our Boston page.

Questions couples ask about Willowdale Estate

Have you photographed a wedding at Willowdale Estate?

Not yet — and we say so. We scout Willowdale Estate before your wedding: ceremony light, where formals hold up, how long the walk between spaces really takes.

Is there a travel fee for a wedding in Boston, MA?

No. Elizabeth Scott is a national studio and travel is built into every collection, so a wedding at Willowdale Estate costs the same as one across the street from us.

How far in advance should we book Willowdale Estate photography?

Most Boston couples book eight to twelve months out; peak summer and autumn dates go first. A signed agreement and the retainer hold your date, and the balance is due two weeks before the wedding.

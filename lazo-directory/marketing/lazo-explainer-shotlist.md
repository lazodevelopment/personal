# Lazo — Explainer Video Script

**Length:** ~90 seconds (≈220 words of voiceover)
**Format:** 16:9 master, safe-framed for a 9:16 cut
**Tone:** warm, calm, a little wry. Confident, never salesy.
**VO:** one narrator. June's lines use her real ElevenLabs voice (Lily).

---

## 0:00–0:08 · The hook

**VISUAL:** Close-up of a hand with a new engagement ring holding a phone. Notifications stack up: "Have you set a date??", "Who's doing photos?", "Send me the budget". The phone buzzes again.

**VO:**
You just got engaged. Congratulations.
Now everyone has questions, and you have about four hundred tabs open.

---

## 0:08–0:20 · The problem

**VISUAL:** Quick cuts of a cluttered screen: a spreadsheet, a directory page full of "Sponsored" badges, an inbox filling with vendor emails after one inquiry.

**VO:**
Most wedding sites make their money selling *you*, to vendors.
Send one inquiry and your inbox fills up. The top results? Often the ones who paid to be there.

---

## 0:20–0:28 · Meet Lazo

**VISUAL:** Clutter wipes away to a clean white frame. Lazo logo, then the app opening on a phone.

**VO:**
Lazo is wedding planning that works for the couple. It's free, and it starts with June.

---

## 0:28–0:45 · June

**VISUAL:** The June core (the gold-and-plum animated ring) pulses as she speaks. On screen, a checklist, a priced budget and a wedding website build themselves line by line.

**JUNE (her voice):**
Tell me your city, your date and roughly how many guests.

**VO:**
In about the time it takes to answer one more text, June builds your checklist, prices every line of your wedding from what couples in your city actually pay, and keeps the math current as you book.

---

## 0:45–1:00 · Vendors you can trust

**VISUAL:** A Lazo vendor page. Zoom on a "Lazo Score" and a review tagged "Verified wedding". A single message sends to one photographer, with a "Sent to 1 vendor" confirmation.

**VO:**
When you're ready to book, every review on Lazo comes from a couple who proved the wedding happened.
Rankings are the Lazo Score and nothing else, so nobody can buy the top spot.
And when you message a vendor, it goes to that one vendor. Not to a lead list.

---

## 1:00–1:12 · Your wedding website

**VISUAL:** Scroll through three or four of the live designs, then a guest's phone: RSVP, table lookup, hotel block, uploading a photo on the day.

**VO:**
Pick from forty designer wedding websites and go live in about a minute.
Guests RSVP, find their table and the hotel block, and add their own photos. No ads, no upsells, no guest cap.

---

## 1:12–1:20 · Already started elsewhere

**VISUAL:** A guest list exported from another planner drops into Lazo in one motion. A registry icon stays where it is.

**VO:**
Already started somewhere else? Bring your guest list over in one step. Your registry stays right where it is.

---

## 1:20–1:30 · Close and call to action

**VISUAL:** The couple from the opening, now relaxed and laughing over champagne, with the phone face-down on the table. End card: Lazo logo, **meetlazo.com**, App Store badge.

**VO:**
Lazo. Plan the wedding, then get back to the good part.
Start free at meetlazo.com.

## Production notes

- **Claims check:** every claim here comes from live meetlazo.com copy (the /welcome/ pages). "Forty designs", "about a minute" and "verified wedding" reviews should stay in step with the site if any of them change.
- **Never** show getlazo.com. The domain is meetlazo.com.
- **Store badges:** show the App Store badge only. Add Google Play once the Android app is approved.
- **Competitors:** the script hints at the lead-selling model without naming The Knot or Zola. Keep it that way in paid ads, since naming them invites ad-policy and legal review.
- **Screens to capture:** the June hub (june.meetlazo.com), a vendor page that shows the Lazo Score, 3–4 wedding-site designs, the couple dashboard's guest import.

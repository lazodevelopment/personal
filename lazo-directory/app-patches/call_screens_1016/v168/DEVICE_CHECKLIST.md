# Real-device checklist after pasting Couple v168 / Vendor v208

Do this on one iPhone and one Android phone (or one phone plus a laptop), with a real couple account and a real vendor account on the same thread.

1. **Speaking ring, both sides.** Join a call with both cameras off. Talk on one device: that person's avatar ring should light up on both screens. Then talk on the other device and check the same.
2. **Sheets from the thread header on a phone.** In the couple's thread on a phone:
   - tap the video button: "Join with video / Join with audio only" should open on top of the thread, and the thread should stay open behind it;
   - tap "...": the menu should open with "Schedule a Lazo video call" at the top (vendor on Pro or Studio). Tap it and the schedule sheet should open.
   - In the browser harness the thread closed instead. This checks it doesn't on a real phone.
3. **First call from a couple account.** Tap the video button. The phone-code step appears, a real text arrives, and after the code you're in the call.
4. **Locked camera.** The couple sees "<vendor> turns your camera on when they're ready". When the vendor taps "Let them use camera", the couple sees "You can turn your camera on now".
5. **Vendor view.** The couple's video arrives blurred. "Show video" reveals it, "Hide video" hides it again. "Report" leaves the call and opens the report sheet.
6. **Audio only.** The couple taps "Join with audio only". The vendor sees "Prefers audio only" and no camera prompt.
7. **Schedule.** A Pro vendor schedules a call 20 minutes ahead. Both get the card. The couple gets a push and an email. Reminders arrive about 15 minutes before. Join opens 10 minutes before. "Add to calendar" works with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.
8. **Bad network.** Turn on airplane mode for 20 seconds mid-call, then turn it off. The call should recover video within a few seconds, or say "Connection lost. Leave and join again."

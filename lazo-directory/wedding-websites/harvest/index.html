Our story

A cider stand, a wrong turn, and a very good pie.

We met in line at the orchard the October the hayride broke down. Nobody wanted to walk back, so we didn't. Four falls later we are getting married under the same maples, with the people who make our world and as much pie as the kitchen will allow.

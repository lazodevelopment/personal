Questions

How much does a wedding cost?

The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study puts the US average at $34,000 for about 13 vendors. Your number depends mostly on guest count and venue, which is why June asks for those first.

Where do these ranges come from?

They are the ranges Lazo publishes in its cost guides, phrased as what couples actually pay. They are national, not quotes from any vendor.

Is the budget tracker free?

Yes. The budget, the guest list, the seating chart, the website and June are all free for couples. Lazo takes 0% of what you pay your vendors.

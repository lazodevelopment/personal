    Why couples move

    Message one vendor. Hear from one vendor.

    On the big marketplaces an inquiry is sold as a lead, so a question to one florist becomes a week of follow-ups from strangers, and rankings go to whoever pays for placement. On Lazo your message reaches the vendor you chose and no one else, and no vendor can buy a better spot at any price.

    Questions

    Do I lose my registry if I switch?

    No. Your registry stays on Zola, Amazon or wherever it is. Lazo links to it from your website and never marks it up.

    What comes across in the import?

    Names, parties and households, mailing addresses, emails, phone numbers and RSVP status, from any CSV. June can also read a pasted budget or vendor list.

    Can I keep both for a while?

    Yes. Nothing is deleted on the other side. Most couples move the guest list and website first and let the old account expire.

Find a couple's wedding website

Type a name and the couple's site appears: the date, the schedule, where to stay, the registry, and the RSVP. All on one link.

Looking for a wedding website?

How do I find a couple's wedding website?

Type either partner's first name, or both names, in the search above. Public Lazo wedding websites appear as you type. If the couple set a passcode, their site will not appear here; ask them for the link and the passcode.

Why can't I find the wedding website I'm looking for?

Either the couple made their site private with a passcode, they spelled their names differently on the site, or the site is not on Lazo. Check the invitation for the link.

Can I RSVP from the wedding website?

Yes. Open the couple's site, tap RSVP, and reply with your attendance, meal choice and any notes. No account is needed.

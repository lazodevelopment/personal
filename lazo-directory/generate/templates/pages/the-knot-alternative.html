Why couples go looking for an alternative

On advertising-driven directories, the vendor at the top of your search is often the one who paid the most for placement — not the one couples loved most. Reviews are easy to post and hard to trust, and once you inquire, your contact details can end up in more inboxes than you intended. None of that helps you pick the people who will stand next to you on your wedding day.

How Lazo works instead

Rankings are never for sale. Search order comes from the Lazo Score — a reputation number built from verified reviews, weighted for recency. No vendor can buy a better position, ever.

Every review is verified. Before a review counts, the couple proves the wedding actually happened. Vendors respond free and can never pay to bury criticism.

Your info stays yours. Your inquiry goes to the vendor you chose — never to a lead list. We don't sell or share your contact details. That's the entire policy.

Questions about the app

Is the Lazo app free?

Yes, completely. Every planning tool is free for couples, with no subscription, no ads and no guest cap. Lazo's business is an optional Pro plan for vendors, which sharpens their tools and never touches the rankings.

Is Lazo on Android as well as iPhone?

Yes. Lazo: Wedding Planner is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android, and the same plan is at app.meetlazo.com in any browser. Everything stays in sync.

Can my partner use the app too?

Yes. One wedding, two phones: your partner signs in to the same plan and sees the same guest list, budget, vendor threads and website. Nothing to forward.

Can I bring my guest list from The Knot or Zola?

Yes. Export it as a CSV and import it in one step. June can also read a pasted budget or vendor list. Registry links stay wherever they are.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. The full process is here.

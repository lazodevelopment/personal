Different tools, different problems

A CRM organizes your pipeline; it doesn’t fill it. You still pay a monthly subscription while sourcing every client yourself — through ads, referrals, or directories that charge for the privilege. The most expensive part of your business is still finding the next couple.

Where Lazo fits

Demand, not just management. Lazo is a verified wedding marketplace: couples plan here, search here, and inquire here — and their inquiry lands in your inbox with the date attached, plus an SMS so you can answer first.

Proposals and booking, built in. Send price and terms in the thread; the couple accepts in one tap. For many vendors that covers the proposal-and-booking slice of what a paid CRM does — without the subscription.

A reputation engine on top. Verified reviews build your Lazo Score, and the score sets your search ranking. Your marketing compounds instead of renting.

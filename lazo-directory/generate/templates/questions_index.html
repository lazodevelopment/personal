How to actually use these

  • Ask by email where you can. A written answer is a record, and vendors are more precise in writing. Verbal assurances about overtime and cancellation have a way of evaporating.
  • Ask the awkward ones anyway. Referral fees, backup plans and what happens if they cannot make it are exactly the questions people skip because they feel rude. A professional expects them.
  • Notice how they answer, not just what they answer. A vendor who is annoyed you asked about the cancellation clause is showing you how they will behave when something goes wrong.
  • Get the answers into the contract. A promise made in an email is worth far less than the same promise as a clause. If it matters, ask for it in writing in the agreement.

Why our prices aren't sponsored

Most wedding sites earn money from the vendors they list, which is why their "recommended" {{ cat.label|lower }} and their cost advice tend to point the same direction. Lazo takes nothing per lead and nothing per booking, and rankings can't be bought at any price. Every review comes from a couple who proved they actually got married with that vendor. So the ranges above are what we believe couples pay — not what anyone paid us to say.

Most wedding planning advice is written to be reassuring. These are written to be useful, which sometimes means telling you that a receiving line costs you twenty-five minutes nobody scheduled, that marriage licenses expire, and that the fastest way to lose an hour is family photographs without a written list.

Every guide ends with what to do about it and which vendor to tell — because a planning problem you cannot hand to anyone is just a worry.

Why these read differently

Lazo earns nothing from vendors — no fee per lead, no fee per booking, and rankings that can't be bought at any price. That means we have no reason to smooth over the parts of wedding planning that are genuinely annoying, and no reason to recommend spending where spending does not help. Where we think something is not worth the money, these guides say so.

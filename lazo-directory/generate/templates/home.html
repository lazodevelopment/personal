Yes. There is no paid tier for couples and nothing is capped. Lazo takes 0% of what couples pay vendors. The business is an optional Pro plan for vendors who want more tools, which sharpens the tools and never touches the rankings, so the couple tools stay free and the rankings stay honest.

By the Lazo Score, built only from verified reviews from couples who proved the wedding happened, weighted for recency. No vendor can pay for a better position.

How much does wedding transportation cost?

Most couples pay $400-$1,200. Here is what moves that number, what you get at each level, and where to spend less without regretting it.

All cost guides

Wedding transportation typically runs $400 to $1,200 depending on the vehicle and the hours, with classic cars and limousines usually carrying three or four-hour minimums. Most couples underspend here and then discover that moving 120 guests between a ceremony and a reception is a logistics problem, not a style choice.

What you get at each price

Couple only

$300-$700

A car for the two of you between venues and away at the end. Usually a three-hour minimum whether you need three hours or not.

What most couples pay most couples

$700-$1,500

A vehicle for the couple plus a shuttle or party bus for the wedding party, or a guest shuttle running between hotel and venue.

Full guest transport

$1,500-$5,000+

Multiple shuttles running loops for all guests across the evening, common at venues with limited parking or where guests are drinking.

These are what couples actually pay, not quotes we can make on a vendor's behalf. Every transportation company sets their own prices, and the ones on Lazo publish theirs where they choose to.

What actually drives the price

Hourly minimums
Three to four hours is standard even for a twenty-minute journey, and the clock often starts at the depot.
Vehicle type
A sedan, a stretch limousine, a vintage car and a 40-seat coach are four different prices for the same twenty minutes.
Number of runs
A shuttle doing three loops is priced very differently from one doing a single transfer.
Late-night returns
The end-of-night run is where most couples add cost, and it is usually the one guests most need.
Peak Saturdays
Prom season and peak wedding Saturdays genuinely sell out the local fleet.
Gratuity
Frequently added automatically at 18-20%.

How to spend less without regretting it

Shuttle guests, not yourselves
If you have to choose, moving guests safely matters more than arriving in something photogenic.
Run loops instead of one big coach
A smaller vehicle doing three trips often costs less than a coach doing one, and starts the party earlier for whoever is on it.
Skip the getaway car
It is a lovely photograph and a full hourly minimum.
Use a rideshare arrangement for the late run
Some venues and hotels will coordinate a rideshare code instead of a contracted late shuttle.

When to book

Four to eight months out, and earlier for peak Saturdays or if you need a specific vintage vehicle - most operators own only one.

What to ask before you book

  • What is the minimum, and when does the clock start - at pickup or at the depot?
  • Is gratuity included, and at what rate?
  • What is the backup if a vehicle breaks down on the day?
  • Can the vehicle actually access my venue - low bridges, narrow lanes, parking?
  • How many runs are included, and what does an extra late-night loop cost?

Common questions

How much does wedding transportation cost?

Wedding transportation typically costs $400 to $1,200 for the couple and wedding party, with three or four-hour minimums common. Full guest shuttles running multiple loops across the evening run $1,500 to $5,000 or more depending on vehicle size and number of runs.

Do we need to provide transportation for guests?

You are not obliged to, but it matters most where parking is limited, the venue is remote, or you are serving alcohol and guests would otherwise drive. A shuttle between the hotel and venue is one of the more appreciated things couples pay for.

How long do we need to book a wedding car for?

Most operators have a three or four-hour minimum regardless of how long the journey takes, and the clock frequently starts when the vehicle leaves the depot rather than when it reaches you. Ask which, because it changes the real cost.

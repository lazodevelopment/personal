How much does day-of coordination cost?

Most couples pay $800-$2,000. Here is what moves that number, what you get at each level, and where to spend less without regretting it.

All cost guides

Day-of coordination commonly runs $800 to $2,000 - and the name is misleading in a way that matters. A good coordinator starts four to six weeks before the wedding: confirming every vendor, building the timeline, and running the rehearsal and the day itself. It is the cheapest tier of wedding planning and the one couples most often say they would not have gone without.

What you get at each price

Minimal

$500-$800

Someone present on the day to execute a timeline you wrote. Better than nothing, but you are still doing the coordination.

Premium / complex

These are what couples actually pay, not quotes we can make on a vendor's behalf. Every coordinator sets their own prices, and the ones on Lazo publish theirs where they choose to.

What actually drives the price

When they actually start
Four to six weeks out versus the morning of is the entire difference in this category. Ask directly.
Guest count and venue complexity
Two hundred guests, two locations, or a bare site all need more hands.
Team size
An assistant is typically $300-$600 and is what allows one person to be at the ceremony while another sets the reception.
Hours on the day
Most packages cover 10-12 hours. Setup starting at 8am and strike ending at midnight exceeds that.
Rehearsal attendance
Usually included at this tier, but confirm.

How to spend less without regretting it

This is the saving
Coordination is the line that protects every other line. The alternative is not saving $1,200 - it is your mother or your maid of honour working your wedding instead of attending it.
Check the venue's offer honestly
Some venues include real coordination. Most include a building manager. Ask what they do about your florist being late, and listen to the answer.
Take the single coordinator if the day is simple
Under 100 guests at one venue rarely needs a second person.
Do your own design
Coordination does not include styling. If you have that covered, you do not need to move up a tier.

When to book

Four to eight months out. Coordinators book later than full-service planners, but the well-regarded ones in each market still fill peak Saturdays a year ahead.

What to ask before you book

  • When exactly do you take over, and what happens between now and then?
  • Do you contact and confirm all of my vendors, or do I?
  • How many hours on the day, and does that cover setup and strike?
  • Will you run the rehearsal?
  • How many weddings do you have that weekend, and who is my backup?

Common questions

How much does day-of wedding coordination cost?

Day-of coordination commonly costs $800 to $2,000. Minimal execute-only service starts around $500; complex weddings needing multiple coordinators run $2,000 to $4,000 or more.

What does a day-of coordinator actually do?

Despite the name, most begin four to six weeks before the wedding - confirming every vendor, building and distributing a timeline, running the rehearsal, then managing setup, the day itself and strike. They are the reason nobody in your family has to work your wedding.

Do we need a coordinator if our venue has one?

Often yes. A venue coordinator is responsible for the venue - its staff, its spaces, its rules. A day-of coordinator is responsible for your wedding across every vendor. Ask the venue what they do if your florist arrives late, and judge from the answer.

How much do wedding rentals cost?

Most couples pay $1,500-$5,000. Here is what moves that number, what you get at each level, and where to spend less without regretting it.

All cost guides

Rental budgets vary enormously with guest count and design - many couples land between $1,500 and $5,000 covering tables, chairs, linens and tableware. The trap in this category is that delivery, setup and strike are frequently separate line items, so two quotes that look comparable often are not. Always compare delivered-and-installed totals.

What actually drives the price

Guest count
Every place setting is a chair, a linen, a plate, three glasses and flatware. The list scales exactly with the list.
Whether the site has anything
A bare field needs power, flooring, lighting and restrooms before a single table arrives. This is the difference between a $3,000 rental order and a $25,000 one.
Delivery, setup and strike
Often 15-25% of the order, sometimes more for a difficult site or an overnight strike.
Upgrade tiers
Chiavari chairs instead of folding chairs, or a specialty linen instead of standard, is typically $4-$12 per guest each.
Tents
Priced by size, sidewalls, flooring and lighting - and a tent almost always brings power and climate control with it.
Peak weekends
Inventory is finite locally. On a busy Saturday the item you want may simply not exist within a hundred miles.

How to spend less without regretting it

Choose a venue that includes things
A venue $2,000 more expensive that includes tables, chairs, linens and china is usually cheaper overall. This decision is made before you ever talk to a rental company.
Upgrade the chairs, not everything
Chairs are in every wide photograph. Flatware is in none.
Consolidate suppliers
One order delivered once beats three orders delivered three times, each with its own fee.
Confirm what the caterer brings
Caterers frequently include china, glassware and flatware. Couples routinely rent these twice.

When to book

Six to nine months out, and much earlier for tents and anything on a bare site. Final counts are usually adjustable until 2-3 weeks before.

What to ask before you book

  • Can I have the total delivered, set up and struck, not just the item prices?
  • What does my caterer already provide so I do not rent it twice?
  • When do you deliver and collect, and does the venue allow those times?
  • What happens if something arrives damaged or short on the day?
  • Is there a replacement charge for breakages, and at what rate?

Common questions

How much do wedding rentals cost?

Most couples spend $1,500 to $5,000 on rentals covering tables, chairs, linens and tableware for 100-150 guests. Building on a bare site with a tent, flooring, lighting, power and restrooms runs $5,000 to $30,000 or more.

Are delivery and setup included in rental quotes?

Frequently not. Delivery, setup and strike commonly add 15-25% to an order and are often listed separately, so always compare quotes on the delivered-and-installed total rather than the item prices.

What do wedding caterers usually provide?

Many caterers include china, glassware, flatware and sometimes linens in their pricing. Confirm exactly what is covered before placing a rental order, because renting these twice is one of the most common wedding budget mistakes.

Looking for a WeddingWire alternative?

WeddingWire has reviews by the millions — and an advertising business behind the rankings. Lazo asks a harder question: what if every review had to be proven, and no ranking could ever be bought?

Volume isn’t the same as trust

A mountain of reviews only helps if you can believe them. On open-submission platforms, anyone can post, vendors juggle their reputation across ad tiers, and the couple reading it all is left doing forensic work on strangers’ five-stars. (Worth knowing: WeddingWire and The Knot are both operated by The Knot Worldwide — same parent, same advertising model.)

What verification changes

Proof before it counts. Every review on Lazo comes from a couple who verified the work happened — contract or payment record. The Lazo Score is built from nothing else.

Rankings you can take at face value. Search order is the score, weighted for recency. New vendors start at the community baseline of 65 — not zero, not fake five stars — and earn their way from there.

An inquiry, not a lead. When you contact a vendor on Lazo, that vendor gets your message — and nobody else does. Your details are never resold.

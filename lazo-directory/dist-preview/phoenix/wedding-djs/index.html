Wedding DJs in Phoenix: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Phoenix?

Peak season in Phoenix runs October to April. Summer highs routinely pass 105°F, so outdoor ceremonies cluster from October to April; summer weddings move indoors or to early evening.

How far ahead should we book a wedding dj in Phoenix?

Plan on 6-9 months out for a peak-season Saturday. A wedding dj can only take one wedding a day, so popular dates go first.

How much does a wedding dj cost in Phoenix?

Most couples spend $1,000-$2,000. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Phoenix, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding dj for their packages.

How do we get a marriage license in Arizona?

Apply with the Clerk of the Superior Court. The fee is $83 and there is no waiting period. The license is valid for 365 days, and you need 2 witnesses.

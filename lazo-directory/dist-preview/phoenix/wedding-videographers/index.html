Three things to ask every wedding videographer in Phoenix
  1. Is audio from the vows and toasts recorded on lapel mics and a board feed, or just the camera?
  2. How long until the highlight film and the full-length edit arrive, and are raw files included?
  3. Is there an hourly cap, and what happens if the reception runs late?

Wedding Videographers in Phoenix: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Phoenix?

Peak season in Phoenix runs October to April. Summer highs routinely pass 105°F, so outdoor ceremonies cluster from October to April; summer weddings move indoors or to early evening.

How far ahead should we book a wedding videographer in Phoenix?

Plan on 8-12 months out for a peak-season Saturday. A wedding videographer can only take one wedding a day, so popular dates go first.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Phoenix?

Most couples spend $1,200-$2,500. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Phoenix, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding videographer for their packages.

What time is sunset for an April wedding in Phoenix?

Around 6:59 PM on April 15, with golden hour from about 6:25 PM. For portraits in that light, a ceremony near 4:14 PM works.

How do we get a marriage license in Arizona?

Apply with the Clerk of the Superior Court. The fee is $83 and there is no waiting period. The license is valid for 365 days, and you need 2 witnesses.

Planning a wedding in Phoenix

Desert weddings here run on the calendar: the Sonoran sky and saguaro backdrops are the draw, and resort venues in Scottsdale and the foothills around Cave Creek and Carefree host much of the market.

Lazo lists 158 wedding venues across 23 towns in the area; the short list below spreads across the metro so you can compare settings before you tour.

Wedding season

October to April. Summer highs routinely pass 105°F, so outdoor ceremonies cluster from October to April; summer weddings move indoors or to early evening.

Three things to ask every wedding venue in Phoenix
  1. Is the date you want a Saturday in October to April? Those go first here, often a year out.
  2. Does the package include the ceremony site, the coordinator and the rentals, or are those separate lines?
  3. Where do guests go when it is 105 degrees? Ask to see the indoor and shaded options, not just the lawn.

Wedding Venues in Phoenix: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Phoenix?

Peak season in Phoenix runs October to April. Summer highs routinely pass 105°F, so outdoor ceremonies cluster from October to April; summer weddings move indoors or to early evening.

How far ahead should we book a wedding venue in Phoenix?

Plan on 12-18 months out for a peak-season Saturday. A wedding venue can only take one wedding a day, so popular dates go first.

How much does a wedding venue cost in Phoenix?

Most couples spend $3,000-$15,000+. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Phoenix, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding venue for their packages.

What time is sunset for an April wedding in Phoenix?

Around 6:59 PM on April 15, with golden hour from about 6:25 PM. For portraits in that light, a ceremony near 4:14 PM works.

How do we get a marriage license in Arizona?

Apply with the Clerk of the Superior Court. The fee is $83 and there is no waiting period. The license is valid for 365 days, and you need 2 witnesses.

The Lazo model

Zero acquisition cost. Profile, leads, SMS alerts, messaging, proposals: free forever. Lazo never charges per lead, so nobody on this platform profits from sending you junk.

Questions vendors ask

What does Lazo charge for?

Never leads, never rankings. Optional paid services are planned, and none of them will ever touch search order.

How do I build my score?

Invite past couples to leave verified reviews — free. Each verified review moves your Lazo Score; recency is weighted, so great recent work counts most.

Can couples find me if I haven’t claimed my page?

Often yes — Lazo lists 3,000+ vendor pages. Claiming makes it yours: photos, pricing display, instant SMS alerts, and proposal tools.

Is there a contract?

No contract, no card, no lock-in. Leave whenever you like — though vendors with free bookings rarely do.

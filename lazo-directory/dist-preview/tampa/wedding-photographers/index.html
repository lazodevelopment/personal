Wedding Photographers in Tampa: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Tampa?

Peak season in Tampa runs January to April and October to December. Fall through spring is the main season; summer is hot with afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November.

How far ahead should we book a wedding photographer in Tampa?

Plan on 9-14 months out for a peak-season Saturday. A wedding photographer can only take one wedding a day, so popular dates go first.

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Tampa?

Most couples spend $1,500-$3,500. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Tampa, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding photographer for their packages.

What time is sunset for an April wedding in Tampa?

Around 7:55 PM on April 15, with golden hour from about 7:23 PM. For portraits in that light, a ceremony near 5:10 PM works.

How do we get a marriage license in Florida?

Apply with the clerk of the circuit court. The fee is $86 and there is a 3-day waiting period. The license is valid for 60 days, and no witnesses are needed.

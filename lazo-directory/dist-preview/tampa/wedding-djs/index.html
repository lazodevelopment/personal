Wedding DJs in Tampa: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Tampa?

Peak season in Tampa runs January to April and October to December. Fall through spring is the main season; summer is hot with afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November.

How far ahead should we book a wedding dj in Tampa?

Plan on 6-9 months out for a peak-season Saturday. A wedding dj can only take one wedding a day, so popular dates go first.

How much does a wedding dj cost in Tampa?

Most couples spend $1,000-$2,000. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Tampa, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding dj for their packages.

How do we get a marriage license in Florida?

Apply with the clerk of the circuit court. The fee is $86 and there is a 3-day waiting period. The license is valid for 60 days, and no witnesses are needed.

Planning a wedding in Tampa

Couples choose between Tampa, St. Petersburg and the Gulf beaches around Clearwater, with Sarasota to the south.

Lazo lists 226 wedding venues across 37 towns in the area; the short list below spreads across the metro so you can compare settings before you tour.

Wedding season

January to April and October to December. Fall through spring is the main season; summer is hot with afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November.

When to book

Book a wedding venue 12-18 months out for a peak-season Saturday. Booking order for every vendor →

Florida marriage license

Three things to ask every wedding venue in Tampa
  1. Where do guests go when the afternoon thunderstorm rolls in or it is 95 degrees in August? Ask to see the indoor or covered plan, not a tent clause.
  2. What is in the rental: how many hours, tables, chairs, a coordinator, and what is a separate line?
  3. What happens to my date and deposit if a hurricane closes the venue between June and November?

Wedding Venues in Tampa: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Tampa?

Peak season in Tampa runs January to April and October to December. Fall through spring is the main season; summer is hot with afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November.

How far ahead should we book a wedding venue in Tampa?

Plan on 12-18 months out for a peak-season Saturday. A wedding venue can only take one wedding a day, so popular dates go first.

How much does a wedding venue cost in Tampa?

Most couples spend $3,000-$15,000+. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Tampa, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding venue for their packages.

What time is sunset for an April wedding in Tampa?

Around 7:55 PM on April 15, with golden hour from about 7:23 PM. For portraits in that light, a ceremony near 5:10 PM works.

How do we get a marriage license in Florida?

Apply with the clerk of the circuit court. The fee is $86 and there is a 3-day waiting period. The license is valid for 60 days, and no witnesses are needed.

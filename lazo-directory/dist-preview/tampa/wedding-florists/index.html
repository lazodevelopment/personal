Three things to ask every wedding florist in Tampa
  1. What survives four hours outdoors in a Florida summer, through the ceremony and cocktail hour, and what wilts?
  2. Do you design for the venue, or deliver arrangements? Installation and teardown are the difference in price.
  3. Can I see a full wedding's florals at a budget like mine, not just a bouquet photo?

Wedding Florists in Tampa: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Tampa?

Peak season in Tampa runs January to April and October to December. Fall through spring is the main season; summer is hot with afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June to November.

How far ahead should we book a wedding florist in Tampa?

Plan on 6-9 months out for a peak-season Saturday.

How much does a wedding florist cost in Tampa?

Most couples spend $1,500-$4,000. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Tampa, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding florist for their packages.

How do we get a marriage license in Florida?

Apply with the clerk of the circuit court. The fee is $86 and there is a 3-day waiting period. The license is valid for 60 days, and no witnesses are needed.

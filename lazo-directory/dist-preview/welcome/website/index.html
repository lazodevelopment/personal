Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. Every design, RSVPs, the schedule, travel details, registry links and passcode privacy are free, with no ads and no premium tier. Lazo's business is an optional Pro plan for vendors.

Can I keep my registry on Zola or Amazon?

Yes. Link any registry from anywhere. Lazo never hosts or marks up a registry.

Can I make the site private?

Yes. Add a passcode and only guests with it can open the site. Public sites can be found by name at meetlazo.com/couples.

Can I bring my guest list from The Knot or Zola?

Yes. Export it as a CSV and import it in one step.

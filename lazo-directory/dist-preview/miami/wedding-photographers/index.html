Wedding Photographers in Miami: questions couples ask

When is wedding season in Miami?

Peak season in Miami runs November to April. The dry season from November to April is the peak; summer brings heat, daily rain and hurricane season from June to November.

How far ahead should we book a wedding photographer in Miami?

Plan on 9-14 months out for a peak-season Saturday. A wedding photographer can only take one wedding a day, so popular dates go first.

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Miami?

Most couples spend $1,500-$3,500. Lazo does not yet hold verified booking prices for Miami, so treat this as a national range and ask each wedding photographer for their packages.

What time is sunset for an April wedding in Miami?

Around 7:43 PM on April 15, with golden hour from about 7:13 PM. For portraits in that light, a ceremony near 4:58 PM works.

How do we get a marriage license in Florida?

Apply with the clerk of the circuit court. The fee is $86 and there is a 3-day waiting period. The license is valid for 60 days, and no witnesses are needed.

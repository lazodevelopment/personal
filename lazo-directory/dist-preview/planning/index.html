Planning

The parts nobody warns you about.

33 guides to the wedding decisions that actually cause trouble — and the specific thing to tell your vendors so they don't.

Most wedding planning advice is written to be reassuring. These are written to be useful, which sometimes means telling you that a receiving line costs you twenty-five minutes nobody scheduled, that marriage licenses expire, and that the fastest way to lose an hour is family photographs without a written list.

Every guide ends with what to do about it and which vendor to tell — because a planning problem you cannot hand to anyone is just a worry.

The day itself

The wedding day timeline What does a wedding day timeline look like? A wedding day timeline is built backwards, not forwards. The ceremony order What is the order of a wedding ceremony? Most wedding ceremonies follow the same seven beats: processional, welcome, readings, vows, rings, pronouncement, recessional. The reception order What is the order of events at a wedding reception? A reception has a shape: entrance, dinner, speeches, first dances, then the floor opens. The first dance How do you choose a first dance song? The practical constraints on a first dance song are shorter than people expect: it wants a steady tempo, a clear beginning, and ideally a length under three and a half minutes. The emergency kit What should be in a wedding emergency kit? A wedding emergency kit solves about a dozen predictable problems: a broken strap, a stain, a headache, a blister, a missing button.

People

The guest list How do you make a wedding guest list? The guest list is the most expensive decision in wedding planning, because catering, bar, rentals, cake, invitations and transport are all priced per person. Plus-ones Who gets a plus-one at a wedding? There is no universal rule about plus-ones, which is exactly why you need your own. Kids at weddings Should you have kids at your wedding? Either choice is entirely normal, and both need to be communicated clearly and early. The seating chart How do you make a wedding seating chart? Do not start the seating chart until RSVPs are closed and the final count is in, because every change cascades. The wedding party How do you choose your wedding party? Choose people for who they are on a hard day, not for who has the longest history with you.

The wedding party

Maid of honor duties What are the maid of honor's duties? The maid of honour's real job is not on the list anyone hands you. Bridesmaid duties What are a bridesmaid's duties? Buy the dress, turn up to the things, and on the day be useful without being asked. Best man duties What are the best man's duties? Hold the rings, give the speech, organise the bachelor party, and on the day keep the groom fed, on time and calm.

Words

Writing your own vows How do you write your own wedding vows? Personal vows work when they are specific and short. Wedding speeches How do you write a wedding speech? Five minutes. Invitation wording How do you word a wedding invitation? A wedding invitation has to convey five things: who is hosting, who is marrying, when, where, and what to do next. Thank-you notes When should you send wedding thank-you notes? Within three months of the wedding, and within two weeks for gifts that arrive beforehand.

Money & who pays

Who pays for the wedding Who pays for the wedding? In the United States today, most weddings are paid for by some combination of the couple and both sets of parents, and the couple is usually the largest single contributor. Who pays for the rehearsal dinner Who pays for the rehearsal dinner? Traditionally the groom's family, and this is one of the few old conventions that has largely survived. Who pays for showers & parties Who pays for the bridal shower and bachelorette party? The bridal shower is hosted and paid for by whoever throws it — traditionally the maid of honour and bridesmaids, sometimes a relative. What a wedding dress costs How much does a wedding dress cost? Most wedding dresses cost between $1,200 and $2,500, and the number that catches people out is alterations — commonly $300 to $800 on top, almost never included, and required on nearly every dress. What rings cost How much does a wedding ring cost? Wedding bands typically cost $500 to $1,500 each, and engagement rings $2,000 to $8,000 depending almost entirely on the centre stone.

Logistics

The marriage license How do you get a marriage license? Both of you apply in person at a county clerk's office, bring photo ID, pay a fee usually between $30 and $120, and receive the license. Managing RSVPs How do you manage wedding RSVPs? Set your RSVP deadline three to four weeks before the wedding, roughly a week before your caterer's final count is due. The rehearsal dinner Who is invited to the rehearsal dinner? At minimum: the wedding party and their partners, immediate family, the officiant, and anyone with a role in the ceremony. The rain plan What is a wedding rain plan? A rain plan is not 'we'll move inside'. Your wedding website What should you put on your wedding website? A wedding website exists to answer the questions guests would otherwise text you. Changing your name How do you change your name after marriage? The order is fixed and it matters: certified marriage certificate first, then Social Security, then your driver's licence, then passport, then banks and everything else.

Your wedding website

What goes on the website What should we put on our wedding website? Put on your wedding website every question a guest would otherwise text you, in the order they will ask it: when and where, how to get there and where to stay, what to wear, how to reply and by when, and where the registry is. How to make a wedding website How do we make a wedding website? Making a wedding website takes about twenty minutes if you do it in the right order: pick a design, type your names and date, add the venue and the schedule, publish, and share the link. Wedding website wording What should our wedding website say? Write your wedding website the way you would text a friend who is coming: short, warm, and specific. When to share the website When should we send out our wedding website? Share your wedding website with the save-the-date, six to eight months before the wedding, or ten to twelve for a destination wedding, with the date, the city and where to stay already on it.

The best wedding planning apps What are the best wedding planning apps? The best wedding planning app is the one that does the job you actually have this month: finding vendors you can trust, keeping the guest list and budget in one place, and building a website that answers your guests' questions.

The honest comparison

Looking for a Folia alternative?

Folia is a tidy planner with a subscription and a guest cap. Lazo is the same tools and more — guest list, budget, both seating charts, dream board, timeline, website — free forever, no card, no cap, with a verified vendor marketplace and June behind them.

The same planner, without the meter running

Folia’s free plan is generous until it isn’t: 500 guests, 10 vendors, and the two features most couples actually come for — ceremony seating and reception seating — sit behind $11.99 a month, $89 a year or $129 for two years. Lazo has no paid tier for couples. The seating charts, the dream board, the timeline, the guest list and the budget are free the day you sign up and free the day after the wedding, when the site flips to “We’re married” and keeps going.

Where Lazo goes further

Vendors you can trust, in the same place. Folia tracks the vendors you found elsewhere. Lazo is where you find them: 3,000+ vendor pages ranked only by verified couple reviews, with proposals, contracts and card payments inside the thread.

The website feeds the plan. Guests RSVP on your free Lazo website and land in the guest list with their meals, which show up on the seating chart table by table. Nothing is typed twice.

June. An AI planner who knows your date, budget, guest list and bookings. She seats everyone from your parties and sides, drafts vendor messages, and tells you what to book next and whether it is running late.

Lazo is free for couples with no caps. Folia’s free plan stops at 500 guests and 10 vendors and keeps both seating charts behind a subscription. On Lazo every couple tool is free forever, with no card and no ads.

Does Lazo have ceremony seating?

Yes. Rows on both sides of the aisle named for each of you, reserved front rows for parents and family, a processional planner, and an usher list to print.

Yes. Pin from any link including Pinterest and Etsy, upload photos, add notes and colors, comment with your partner, and share it with your florist and planner.

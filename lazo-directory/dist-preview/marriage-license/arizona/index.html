Arizona marriage license: cost, where to apply, and when

Issued by the clerk of the superior court. Valid a full year anywhere in Arizona. Both of you appear with photo ID.

When to apply, for your date

The license lasts 365 days and there is no wait, so apply any time in the 365 days before the wedding, including the day itself. Enter your date and we will do the arithmetic.

Where to apply

The offices that serve the Arizona cities Lazo covers. Both of you go, with government photo ID; check the office's own page for hours, appointments and what to bring.

Maricopa County · Clerk of the Superior CourtPhoenix · about $83 · Several locations; some take walk-ins, others need an appointment.www.clerkofcourt.maricopa.gov ↗ Pima County · Clerk of the Superior CourtTucson · about $83www.sc.pima.gov ↗

What to bring

Both of you, in person, with government photo ID such as a driver's license or passport. Know your parents' full names and birthplaces, which most applications ask. If either of you was married before, know the date and how it ended; some offices ask for the decree. Bring the fee; not every office takes cards.

Arizona marriage license FAQ

How much does a marriage license cost in Arizona?

About $83, paid to the clerk of the superior court. Fees change and differ by county; confirm with the office.

Where do you get a marriage license in Arizona?

From the clerk of the superior court. Valid a full year anywhere in Arizona. Both of you appear with photo ID.

Is there a waiting period in Arizona?

No waiting period. You can marry the day the license is issued.

How long is a Arizona marriage license valid?

Valid 1 year. Apply within 365 days of the wedding, after any waiting period.

Do you need witnesses in Arizona?

Yes, 2 witnesses over 18 sign at the ceremony.

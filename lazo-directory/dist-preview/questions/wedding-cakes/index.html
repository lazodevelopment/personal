Questions to ask

Questions to ask a wedding cake baker

Not just what to ask — what a good answer sounds like, and what should worry you.

All vendor questions

Cake is a small line with two reliable surprises in it: what you are actually buying is servings rather than tiers, and your venue may charge more to cut it than you expect.

These are specific to wedding cakes & desserts. The questions worth asking every vendor — contracts, deposits, cancellation, insurance and referral fees — are on the main questions page, and you should ask those too.

What you are buying

How many servings is this, and at what slice size?

What to listen forServing sizes vary between bakers, so 'serves 120' is not a fixed quantity. Ask for the slice dimensions - a generous baker and a stingy one can quote the same number for very different amounts of cake.

Does my venue charge a cutting fee, and have you worked there?

What to listen forAt $3 a slice for 150 guests that is $450, which can exceed the cake. A baker who regularly works your venue will know the fee before you do.

Can we do a display cake with kitchen sheet cakes?

What to listen forAny baker should offer this, and it typically saves 40-60%. A refusal to discuss it is a sign you are being sold up.

Whether it survives the day

How will this hold up in the temperature at my venue?

What to listen forButtercream in an August outdoor reception is a real structural question. A baker who asks about the venue, the time of day and whether the cake sits in sun is one who has had a cake fail.

When do you deliver, who sets it up, and what is the plan if it is damaged?

What to listen forTiered cakes are assembled on site. Ask whether they stay to assemble, how late they deliver, and what happens if something goes wrong in the van.

Taste, which is somehow last

Can we taste the exact flavour and filling combination we are ordering?

What to listen forTasting boxes often contain the baker's standard range, not your chosen combination. If you want an unusual pairing, ask to taste that specifically.

Can you handle allergies safely, or is there cross-contamination?

What to listen forA small bakery with one kitchen may not be able to guarantee a gluten-free or nut-free cake. An honest baker says so rather than promising it.

Common questions

What should I ask a wedding cake baker?

Ask how many servings you are buying and at what slice size, whether your venue charges a cutting fee, whether you can do a display cake with kitchen sheet cakes, and how the cake will hold up in your venue's temperature.

What is a cake cutting fee?

A charge some venues apply, typically $1-$4 per slice, to cut and serve a cake they did not supply. On a 150-guest wedding that can cost more than the cake, so confirm it before ordering.

Do wedding cake tastings include our chosen flavours?

Not always - tasting boxes often contain the baker's standard range rather than your specific flavour and filling combination. If you want something unusual, ask to taste that exact pairing.

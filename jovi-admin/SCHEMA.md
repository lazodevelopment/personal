# Jovi (Firebase project `kurv-health`) — Firestore data dictionary for the staff app

Extracted 2026-09-23 from the 44 FlutterFlow widgets + functions/notifications.js. `assets/js/data.js` encodes this; keep both in sync with the app.

## Conventions
- Owner key on top-level collections is `userId` (uid). `security_logs` uses `user_id`. Cancelled requests move the uid to `previousUserId` and set `userId: null`. Pet claims `memberId` = uid; human claims `memberId` = 'primary' | 'spouse' | dependent memberId.
- Dates are Firestore Timestamps except: `requests.appointmentDate` ('yyyy-MM-ddT00:00:00', local) + `requests.appointmentTime` ('h:mm AM/PM' e.g. '9:30 AM'), human DOB strings (`birth` 'MM-dd-yyyy', `dob` free text, dependents 'MM/dd/yyyy'), legacy pet `birthdate`.
- `users.deps`, `users.dependents`, `users.members`, `users.pets` are arrays of **JSON strings**, not maps. Keep writing strings.
- Functions lowercase `status` before comparing. Slot blocking in the app counts only `pending` and `confirmed` — staff must confirm with `confirmed`.
- Clinics are hard-coded in the app (no `clinics` collection): `requests.clinic` ∈ 'Littleton - Dakota Ridge', 'Scottsdale - Scottsdale Gateway', 'Dallas - Medical City Campus'. Human slots 9:00 AM–4:30 PM every 30 min; pet slots 8:00 AM–5:30 PM.
- Staff identity in the member app's chat: `users/{uid}.role == 'team'`. Staff app roles live in `staff/{uid}`.
- Known app bugs to work around: AM/PM ignored by the reminder function and Care Records; Clinic Locator compares `clinic` to the short name; Home's help-ticket badge queries fields nobody writes (`createdBy`, `senderRole`, `read`); human claim statuses are capitalised ('Pending', 'Processing'); Home looks for `needs_info` while functions use `needs_more_info`.
- SSNs are plaintext on the user doc (`ssn`, `spouseSsn`, `dependents[].ssn`). The staff app never renders them.

### users/{uid}/claims/{id} (human)
`memberId` ('primary'|'spouse'|dependent memberId), `type` ('receipt' = reimburse member | 'claim' = pay provider), `amount` (int dollars), `date` (TS service date), `photoUrl`, `status` ('Pending','Processing' capitalised on create; then draft, under_review, needs_more_info, approved, paid, rejected, denied), `correspondences[]` (`{id,url,description,uploadedAt,type}`), `provider`, `reason`, `submittedAt`, `paymentProcessed`, `paymentId`, `paidAmount`. Triggers: onHumanClaimCreated (skips draft), onHumanClaimUpdated (status change).

## requests/{id} (appointments)
`id` (random, ≠ doc id), `userId` (null after member cancel), `timezone`, `audience` ('Pet' for pets), `visitType` (human 'Urgent Care','Primary Care','Wellness'; pet 'Wellness','Sick or Injured'), `visitMode` ('Clinic'|'Virtual'), `clinic`, `patientName`, `symptom`, `symptomDuration`, `details`, `medication`, `selectedBeverage`, `selectedTeaType`, medical snapshot (`currentMedications`,`allergies` str,`previousSurgeries`,`familyMedicalHistory`,`primaryPhysician`,`smokingStatus`,`alcoholConsumption`,`exerciseFrequency`,`dietaryRestrictions`), `appointmentDate`, `appointmentTime`, `photoUrl`, `status` (pending → confirmed/scheduled/booked/rescheduled/completed/no_show/missed/cancelled/canceled/declined; member cancel → 'available' with `userId` null, `previousUserId`, `previousStatus`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy`), `priority`, `kurvPassPurchased`, `estimatedWaitTime`, `createdAt`, `rescheduledAt`, `reminders{h24,h1}`; pet: `petId`,`petName`,`petSpecies`,`petBreed`,`petWeightLbs`,`petDateOfBirth`,`petPhotoUrl`. Staff-written fields Care Records reads: `startAt`, `durationMinutes`, `providerName`, `providerCredentials`, `clinicName`, `clinicAddress`, `clinicPhone`, `reason`, `notes`, `telehealthUrl`, `isTelehealth`, `linkedVisitRecordId`, `updatedAt`, `cancelledReason`. Triggers: onRequestCreated, onRequestUpdated (status/date change), appointmentReminders.
`cancelled_appointments/{id}`: `originalAppointmentId`, `userId`, `patientName`, `appointmentDate`, `appointmentTime`, `visitType`, `visitMode`, `clinic`, `symptom`, `wasPriority`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy` ('user'/'member' = by member, anything else = by clinic), `source`. Trigger onAppointmentCancelled.
`kurv_pass_cancellations/{id}`: `appointmentId`, `userId`, `cancelledAt`, `appointmentDate`, `appointmentTime`, `visitType`, `visitMode`, `clinic`, `nonRefundableAmount` 49, `patientName`.

## prescriptions/{id} (top-level, EHR-written)
`userId`, `medicationName`, `dosage`, `strength`, `frequency`, `prescribedBy`, `refillsRemaining`, `totalRefills`, `daysSupply` (30), `nextRefillDate`, `lastRefilled`, `status` ('active','pending','completed'; also discontinued/expired), `urgent`, `rxNumber`, `instructions`, `category`, `lastFilledPharmacy`, `originalPharmacy`, `prescribedAt`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`. App refill: refillsRemaining−1, lastRefilled, lastFilledPharmacy, nextRefillDate = now + daysSupply.
## prescriptionRefills/{id}
`userId`, `prescriptionId`, `rxNumber`, `medicationName`, `refillDate`, `requestedDate`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, `pharmacyId` (pharmacies id or 'place:{placeId}'), `pharmacyName`, `status` ('requested' → approved/processing/in_progress/ready/filled/completed/denied/rejected/cancelled), `refillMethod`, `paymentPreference` ('goodrx_coupon'|'insurance'), `payWithGoodRx`, `refillNumber`. Trigger onRefillUpdated.
## refills/{id} (Request Care medication refill)
`userId`, `patientName`, `medicationType`, `medicationName`, `currentMedications`, `allergies`, `primaryPhysician`, `status` ('pending' → approved/filled/completed/denied/rejected), `type` 'refill', `createdAt`.
## pharmacies/{id}
`name`, `isActive`, `address` (string or map), `lat`/`lng`, `phone`, `website`, `hours` (7 strings Mon..Sun, or map by weekday).
## helpTickets/{id} (+ messages)
`userId`, `userName`, `phone`, `email`, `issueType`, `issueDescription`, `contactMethod`, `status` ('open'|'closed'), `priority` (0 critical … 3 low), `priorityLevel`, `responseTime`, `hasKurvPass`, `isPremium`, `createdAt`, `assignedTo`, `assignedAt`, `agentTyping`. Messages: `senderId`, `text`, `timestamp`, `readByUser`, `readByTeam`, `imageUrl`, `fileUrl`, `fileName`. Trigger onSupportMessage (skips when senderId == ticket.userId).
## payment_logs/{id} (PayArc webhook)
`userId`, `type`, `status` ('success'|'failed'), `amount`, `reason`, `attemptNumber`, `timestamp`. Trigger onPaymentLogged.
## security_logs/{id}
`event`, `user_id`, `email`, `timestamp`, `reason`, `authentication_method`, `success`.
## Storage
`uploads/{uid}/claims/{claimId}.jpg`, `uploads/{uid}/correspondences/{claimId}/{millis}.jpg`, `uploads/{uid}/pet_claims/...`, `uploads/{uid}/{uuid}.{ext}`, `pet_photos/{uid}/{petId}/{uuid}.jpg`, `users/{uid}/pets/{petId}/meds/{medId}.png`, `request_photos/{uuid}.jpg`, `helpTickets/{ticketId}/{millis}.jpg`.

About Jovi

We think healthcare should feel good, not just be good.

Jovi started with a simple frustration: paying outrageous premiums and still not knowing what a visit would cost. So we built a membership that puts the price up front, lets you see anyone, and treats you like a person instead of a policy number.

Transparent by design

Clear, upfront pricing and no fine print. You always know what you pay, before you pay it.

Real, licensed providers

Care from actual medical professionals, never a bot or a call center. A care team that knows you by name.

Your data stays private

Your health information is encrypted, never sold, and you can export it or delete your account from the app at any time.

Where we are

Jovi memberships are available across all three launch states today, and Jovi Clinics are opening in each as our community grows. Members are the first to know when a clinic opens near them.

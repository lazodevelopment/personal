Privacy policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026. This policy explains how Roven HR, LLC ("Roven," "we") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our websites and applications (the "Platform"). It's written to be read. It is incorporated into our Terms of Service.

1. What we collect

Account basics: name, email address, password (stored as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider — we never see it), and account type.

Candidate profile data: your work history — either a résumé you upload or history you type in — plus the roles you want, preferred metro, employment-type preferences, remote preference, and an optional profile photo.

Employer data: business name, website, industry, metro, your role at the business, and information generated by verification.

Activity data: applications you submit or receive, statuses and timestamps, interview proposals and scheduled times, fit scores and any "why I'm a fit" statement you write, hire confirmations, and listing lifecycle events (posted, confirmed open, paused, closed).

Technical data: standard logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) collected by our hosting and security providers to operate and protect the Platform. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

2. How we use it — including AI processing

We use information to operate the marketplace: matching candidates to roles, delivering applications to employers, coordinating interviews, computing the public employer metrics described in our Terms, sending the emails the Platform runs on (job matches, interview requests, hire confirmations, listing freshness checks), and securing the Platform.

AI processing, plainly: two features use a third-party AI model (Anthropic's Claude). (1) When a candidate uploads a résumé, the document is processed by the model to extract a structured work history — which the candidate reviews and controls. (2) When an employer uses the drafting assistant, their rough notes and role details are processed to generate an editable job description. In both cases the data is sent for processing only, is not used by us to train AI models, and the output belongs to the user who requested it. Match scoring between candidates and roles is performed by our own systems.

We do not sell personal information. We do not rent candidate lists. Employers cannot browse or search a résumé database — candidates are only visible to an employer through an application the candidate chose to submit.

3. What other users see

When a candidate applies to a role, that employer receives the candidate's name, photo (if provided), work history, fit information (including any "why I'm a fit" statement), and application activity. Applying is the act of sharing — nothing is visible to employers before it.

Employers' public information includes their business name, industry, metro, verified status, listings, and the accountability metrics computed from their activity (response rate, response speed, hires), which are displayed publicly by design and by consent under our Terms.

Verified work history: when both sides confirm a hire, a verified entry appears on the candidate's profile.

4. Service providers

We share data only with the processors that run the Platform: Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions), Google Cloud (infrastructure), Cloudflare (hosting, networking, security), Resend (transactional email), Anthropic (AI processing described above), and — when fees activate — a payment processor for employer billing. Each receives only what its function requires. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the Platform and its users, or in connection with a business transfer (with notice).

5. Retention and deletion

We keep information while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Account → Delete): your sign-in is deleted immediately; your profile, work history, résumé files, and photos are purged from active systems promptly (within 30 days at most, typically immediately); and your past applications are anonymized — employers keep the record that an application occurred and how they handled it, without your identity. One exception, for the integrity of verification: confirmed placement records (the fact that a verified hire occurred) are retained in minimal or de-identified form, because verified work histories and employer hiring records must remain trustworthy even when accounts leave. Routine backups age out on a rolling basis.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure providers. Access is controlled by security rules enforced at the database layer, secrets are held in managed secret storage, and sensitive operations (verification, hire confirmation, account deletion) run only through server-side code. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile, work history, preferences, and photo in the app at any time; delete your account as described above; and unsubscribe from non-essential email. Depending on where you live (including California and other states with privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them — email privacy@rovenhr.com and we will honor verified requests within the timelines the law sets. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

8. Children

The Platform is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we'll post the update with a new effective date and, for significant changes, notify you via the Platform or email. Continued use after changes take effect is acceptance.

6.4 Guarantee. If a Hire ends within 30 days of the Hire date, the Placement Fee for that Hire is credited in full toward the Employer's next placement.

This process exists so that no stale listing is presented as open, and Employers agree not to attempt to keep filled or nonexistent roles listed.

Questions employers ask

Straight answers.

Can I pay to have my listing seen first?

No, and we will not build it. Visibility on Roven cannot be purchased by anyone, at any price. Roles surface on fit. The moment placement is for sale, every listing becomes a question about budget rather than about the job - which is the problem we exist to fix.

What does verification require?

Basic business details — legal name, EIN or registration info — run through business identity verification. Most employers clear it in minutes. We never ask you to upload documents by hand unless automated checks can't resolve your entity.

When exactly do I pay?

The moment your offer is accepted — not the start date, not a monthly bill. List pricing is $249 for contract, part-time, or temp hires and $499 for full-time W-2. Launch pricing, published today, is $99 and $199. Right now placement fees are waived entirely while the marketplace grows — and the first 100 verified employers lock 50% off list for life ($124.50 and $249.50), on top of 12 months free after pricing activates.

What if the hire doesn't work out?

Every placement carries a guarantee window. If the hire ends inside it, the fee is credited toward your next placement. We only get paid for hires that stick.

What is the accountability score?

A published score computed from your actual behavior on Roven: how often you respond to applicants, how fast, and whether hires are confirmed. It isn't a review site — nothing is written by anonymous strangers, and nothing can be purchased. Respond to your applicants and the score takes care of itself.

Can a bad score be removed or appealed?

Scores can't be edited — by you or by us — because they're computed from the record. What you can always do is change the record: scores weight recent behavior most heavily, so improved responsiveness shows up quickly.

Can I post contract or 1099 roles?

Yes. Roven supports W-2 employment and 1099 contract roles equally — full-time, part-time, contract, and temporary. Every listing carries the same rules regardless of type: verified employer, confirmed open role, and a tracked answer for every applicant. The listing is labeled with its employment type so candidates know exactly what they're applying to.

Are there actually candidates on Roven?

Roven is free for job seekers forever, every listing appears in Google's job results with full structured data, and every applicant is a verified human — no scrapers, no bots, no résumé mills. The candidate side grows daily, and founding employers get concierge support filling their first roles. If we can't deliver applicants, you pay exactly what the platform cost you: nothing.

Why is it free right now?

Because charging you before we've delivered candidates would make us every other job board. The first 100 verified employers lock founding status: fee-free now, fee-free for 12 months after pricing activates, then 50% off list pricing for life — locked in writing in our Terms. After 100, founding terms close permanently and list pricing applies. The numbers are already published above, so nothing ever surprises you.

How it works

A job board can't be fixed. So we built a marketplace.

Job boards make money on posting and clicks, so their product is volume: more listings, more applications, more noise. Roven only earns when a hire actually happens. That single change reorders everything — who's allowed in, what gets posted, and what happens after you hit apply.

The score, precisely

What goes into an accountability score.

No anonymous reviews. No star ratings from strangers. The score is computed from platform activity — things that verifiably happened — and weighted toward recent behavior, so employers who improve see it reflected fast.

  • RESPONSE RATEOf all applications received, how many got a real disposition — interview, offer, or a clear no.
  • RESPONSE TIMEMedian hours from application to first response. Measured automatically, down to the hour.
  • GHOST RATEApplications left unanswered past the response window. The number job boards refuse to show you.
  • HIRE INTEGRITYHires are confirmed from both sides. The record reflects follow-through, not claims.
  • RECENCY WEIGHTINGRecent behavior counts most. Old history fades; current conduct is what the score reports.

Scores are computed by the platform, cannot be edited by anyone — including us — and are never for sale.

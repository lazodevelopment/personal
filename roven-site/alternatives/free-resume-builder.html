Why ATS-safe matters

Pretty resumes get rejected by software.

Single column, always

Two-column layouts are the most common reason an applicant tracking system scrambles a resume before a person sees it. Every Roven template is single-column.

Real text, not pictures of text

Graphics-heavy templates often export as images that parsers can't read at all. Ours stay selectable and machine-readable.

Standard section headings

Parsers look for the words Experience, Education and Skills. Creative alternatives like "Where I've Made an Impact" quietly break them.

Every template is single-column with real selectable text and standard section headings, which is what applicant tracking systems parse reliably. Multi-column designs are the most common reason a resume gets mangled before a human ever reads it.

Anywhere. It's your document. Download it and send it wherever you want - there's no watermark and no restriction.

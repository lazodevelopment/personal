Why the comparison isn't apples to apples

Three things the sticker price hides.

YOU PAY EITHER WAY

Sponsored job ads bill whether or not the role gets filled. A failed search still costs the full ad spend. Roven costs nothing until an offer is accepted.

AGENCY MARKUP NEVER STOPS

A staffing agency's markup applies to every hour worked, for as long as the placement lasts. A one-time fee ends. An hourly uplift doesn't.

TIME IS A REAL COST

Hours spent reading unqualified applications are the cost nobody puts on the invoice. It's also the one a verified, matched pipeline actually reduces.
